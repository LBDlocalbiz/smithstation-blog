How Do You Pack a 5x10 for Alabama Humidity?

Alabama air is warm and humid. Pack and place items with care so paper, wood, and fabric last in long-term storage. You do not need a climate-control claim to pack like the air is damp.

  1. Use sealed plastic bins for photos, documents, and textiles
  2. Keep cardboard off the concrete with pallets or risers
  3. Wrap wood and upholstery
  4. Take bed frames apart and bag the hardware
  5. Set heavy boxes on the bottom of each stack
  6. Keep the bins you may need near the door
  7. Leave a sliver of space at the walls when the load allows it

Organization protects keepsakes. A labeled unit is a unit you can use. A jammed unit is a unit you fear opening.
